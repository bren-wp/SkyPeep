SkyPeep is a hand-illustrated offline arcade adventure from Brendigo.

Fly through six themed worlds with easy one-tap controls. Keep your aviator between the illustrated stone gates, collect coins and gems, and earn precision stars. Choose Breeze, Explorer or Tempest, each with separate score records.

Each flight provides three lives. Collisions cost a life, and occasional heart pickups help you recover. Unlock bird outfits and cosmetic flight trails using currency earned entirely by playing.

No account, ads, trackers, network permissions or real-money purchases.
Artwork and music are bundled for offline play.

The optional share action uses Android's built-in sharing chooser.
